Vladimir Koman Hungarian Football Player and Sepahan FC Member Visited Our Gallery in Isfahan We were truly honored to welcome Mr. Vladimir Koman, Hungarian football player and member of Sepahan FC, to our gallery in Isfahan. His visit was a great source of encouragement and a valuable opportunity to share our passion for Persian art, culture, and fine jewelry with friends from around the world. We believe that sports, art, and culture connect people and create a more peaceful and beautiful world.

16-india-ambassador.webp
افتخار همکاری با سفیر هند در ایران مایه افتخار و سعادت من است که فرصت همکاری با جناب آقای گادام دهارمندرا (Gaddam Dharmendra)، سفیر هند در ایران را داشتم، که از مشتریان ارزشمند ما بودند. طراحی و ساخت جواهرات سفارشی برای چنین شخصیت برجسته‌ای نه تنها یک افتخار حرفه‌ای، بلکه تجربه‌ای ماندگار در زمینه تعامل فرهنگی بود. این همکاری نشان‌دهنده قدرت هنر، صنعت، دست‌ساز و ارزش‌های مشترک در ایجاد پیوندهای قوی‌تر میان ملت‌ها است. از اعتماد، گفت‌وگوهای ارزشمند و دوستی پایدار ایشان صمیمانه سپاسگزارم.
Honored to Work with the Ambassador of India to Iran I am truly honored to have had the opportunity to work with H.E. Mr. Gaddam Dharmendra, the Ambassador of India to Iran, who was among our valued clients. Designing and crafting bespoke jewelry for such a distinguished diplomat was not only a professional privilege, but also a memorable cultural experience. This collaboration reflects the power of art, craftsmanship, and shared values in building stronger connections between nations. Grateful for the trust, meaningful conversations, and the lasting friendship.
